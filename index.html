Featured Work

Directed Research · Composites · Impact

Riveted Composite Structures Under Static & Impact Loading

CSULB Directed Research (Dr. Surajit Roy): Finite element study of how rivet count (1, 2, and 4) affects damage in aerospace composites. Modeled quasi-static delamination in a 12-ply GFRP laminate (ANSYS ACP with cohesive zone modeling) and 900 m/s impact on a GFRP–Nomex sandwich panel (LS-DYNA with tiebreak contact). Two rivets gave the most balanced static response; four rivets best contained impact damage.

ANSYS ACP + CZM LS-DYNA Explicit −21% Peak Stress (2 vs. 1 Rivet) >50% Less Core Crushing (4 vs. 1 Rivet)
